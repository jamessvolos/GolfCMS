Reading the numbers

SG (strokes gained) — how much your target cost against the caddie's best line. SG −0.30 means that aim gives up about a third of a stroke.

E — expected strokes to hole out from here. "E 3.99 vs caddie 3.02" compares your line's forecast to the optimal one.

The ellipse — your real dispersion: the ball lands somewhere inside it. Rough, sand and trees widen it.

The ring — after each shot, the caddie's optimal target. Green ground on the reveal was smart, red was costly.
