Hole Editor

Load a hole, repaint the ground, certify it, share it as a URL. Tee and cup stay put. To play a real hole: load an aerial image as an underlay, align it (zoom, rotate, move) so the real tee and cup sit on the anchors, then Detect terrain drafts the trace for you — correct what it got wrong, certify, share. The image never leaves your machine and never ships with the course.
